## Shortlist
1. ★ **Windowed re-anneal with the full engine** (II). The engine's own problem class at small n; exact objective (frozen blocks become terminals); legal by construction; also the t ≈ 90 s rescue.
2. **Feasibility homotopy by area scaling** (I). Differs from the failed λ ramp in kind: the constraint set moves, the state stays feasible for the current problem, each step needs only local repair.
3. **Go-with-the-winners + record-to-record acceptance** (III). Attacks the measured variance window directly; builds on the 2×55 restart result.
4. Cheap knobs to fold into Phase F without deepening: directed moves, record-to-record acceptance, proximal anchor, joint shape LP as oracle.

## Deepened

### 1. Windowed re-anneal
- **Sketch.** Grow a window by BFS over geometric adjacency to 10–25 blocks; R = bounding box snapped to block edges (fewest clipped blocks) and to die edges when within half a block. Free = blocks fully inside R; clipped blocks are pinned obstacles; boundary blocks keep their flag only if their edge is an R side on the die edge; MIB groups split by R lock their inside members as fixed-shape. Nets to frozen blocks become fixed terminals at frozen centroids, so the window objective equals the global ΔWL exactly. Inner solve 20k–100k moves at 0.5 µs/pack + window LP; commit only on exact ΔWL < 0 after the global LP. Switch from the global anneal to LNS when the anneal's 5 s improvement rate falls below a 1 s LNS probe (expected 35–50 s).
- **Risk.** Near-rigidity: a 15-block window holds about one block-area of slack, so it may only allow shape trades and equal-area swaps and repeat the < 0.1% single-block result at 100× the cost; if the 1.2–1.6× gap is region-scale misplacement, strict-improvement windows cannot chain the needed migrations.
- **First step.** Pre-test: per-block displacement between a converged and the known-good placement, weighted by WL contribution. Then `extract_window`/`commit_window` with a bit-exact round-trip test and a 200-window histogram of inner feasibility and ΔWL on one converged solution.
- **Children.** Whitespace-gathering LP before freezing; dumbbell/corridor windows for die-scale migration; topological windows (a Γ+ range) with the global pack; rigid super-block lift; ALNS weights over window generators and threshold acceptance at the outer level.

### 2. Feasibility homotopy (area scaling)
- **Sketch.** Scale every movable block's dims by √s (fixed from true dims; soft via a scaled copy of each shape range at the same index so MIB stays identical); preplaced blocks, terminals, outline, flush and the forbidden table stay exact. Phase 1 (0–35 s): the existing strong run at s = 0.92 (relaxed-legal in seconds), post-feasible anneal picks the basin; output, polish and the ttf clock are gated on true feasibility (one extra pack at s = 1). Phase 2 (35–75 s): predictor–corrector — bisect the largest feasible s' (feasibility is monotone in s, ~10 packs), anneal at s' at low T with λ reset and capped until 0.3% slack returns, repeat; a guard forces s = 1 by 75 s. Phase 3: the unchanged tail. The last 1–2% is absorbed by the corrector at s = 1, else by the GLS legalizer (≤ 2% overlap, so the hand-off should be ≈ 1.0×, not 2.1×).
- **Risk.** Compressibility: preplaced blocks, terminals and the outline do not scale, so a 0.96× block may sit in a channel its true size cannot enter; a two-block channel needs a topological move the corrector cannot make, the homotopy stalls at s ≈ 0.97–0.99 and the guard recreates the λ-ramp failure.
- **First step.** `scale_dims(P, S, s)` + `--set s_shrink`; diagnostic before any schedule work: anneal 40 s at fixed s = 0.92 on dev_tight, then (i) bisect s upward with pack alone, (ii) with a 1 s corrector per step; go only if (ii) reaches 1.0 on ≥ 80% of cases with WL at s = 1 within ~5% of the s = 0.92 value.
- **Children.** Two-scale lookahead cost (pack at s plus μ·infeasibility of the same SP at s = 1); shrink only soft blocks; seed-anchored start at the s where the analytical seed's SP is feasible; LP corrector with soft-shape freedom before SA; "breathing" (drop s to 0.98 for 1 s) as a replacement for reheats.

### 3. Go-with-the-winners
- **Sketch.** Legalize once, fork K = 3 Trajectory structs (SP, shapes, RNG, incumbent; ~1–5 KB), round-robin in 10 ms slices (switch overhead < 0.1%); one wall-clock temperature T(t) shared by all, so a clone inherits state and temperature. Every 2 s rank by the best feasible LP-polished incumbent inside the current window (no feasible incumbent = worst); kill the worst and overwrite it with the leader's clone (fresh RNG + ~n/10 forced moves, immune for one checkpoint) only when worst/leader > 1 + ε (ε ≈ 0.03), at most one kill per ~8 s, never below two root lineages before ~35 s. Collapse at 55–60 s; the survivor keeps ≈ 68% of a single run's moves (K = 3), which the 2×55 result prices at under ~1% median. LP polish must be throttled globally (window-best at checkpoints and at the end). Companion: record-to-record travel with δ(t) = κ·T(t)/WL_record as an independent ablation. Ablation grid {K=2,P=55; K=3,P=8; K=4,P=8; K=3 kills off} × {Metropolis, RRT}, scored on max/median and P(final > 1.05·median) over 30 seeds.
- **Risk.** Observability: the scheme pays only if the window incumbent at 18–42 s rank-predicts the final WL; the data brackets this (invisible at first-legal, locked at 50 s) with no interior point. If fate shows only as it freezes (40–50 s), the kills are coin flips and the result is ~1% worse median with no tail cut.
- **First step.** 0.5 s logging of (t, current cost, best feasible polished incumbent) in the single-trajectory engine; 30 seeds × 3–4 cases; Spearman(signal at t_c, final WL) for t_c ∈ {15…50} s. The curve fixes P, ε and t_c before the Trajectory loop (~150 lines) is written.
- **Children.** Self-calibrating ε and t_c from clone-vs-leader gaps; quench-and-rank (20 ms zero-T descent + LP, inherent-structure ranking); late fan-out at 85 s into 4 RNG-distinct cold-phase clones; path-relinking clones (leader's Γ+ with the killed Γ−) re-legalized by GLS; strategy diversity across trajectories as an in-run bandit.

## Provocation
Every branch assumed the search must end at s = 1 in SP space. What if the final 2% were never annealed at all: anneal the *relaxed* problem to the end, and let the exact LP with soft-shape freedom plus the coordinate-space legalizer do all of the tightening in the last 5 s?

## Decisions
- Launch the cheap pre-tests now (displacement analysis; homotopy compressibility diagnostic on a standalone harness).
- Fold directed moves, record-to-record acceptance, GWTW logging into Phase F (fp.cpp, after Phase E lands).
- Windowed LNS only if the displacement pre-test says the gap is local; otherwise its corridor/gathering variants.
